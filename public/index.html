HOW S-H WORKS

Thrift, clearly listed.

Every item can show its measurements, condition, identifiers and current inventory state before you reserve it.

24-hour reservation

An item can stay visible after another customer reserves it, but it cannot be added to another cart. If checkout is not completed within 24 hours, the reservation expires and the item returns automatically.

DELIVERY

Location made clear

Checkout accepts your delivery address, PACI reference and map/location link to make delivery easier to identify.
